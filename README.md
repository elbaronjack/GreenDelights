🌿 **Green Delights**

Green Delights is an addon mod for Farmer's Delight focused on green foods!
Cactus, Bamboo, and Slime are the new main ingredients in your kitchen.

Bring fresh (and slightly weird) flavors to your Minecraft world!


🌵 **Cactus**

Cactus is now more than just a desert decoration!

🌿 Cacti can now be peeled to be eaten.

🔪 Using a cutting board makes the process more efficient (but it's not required).

🤢 **Raw cactus** may cause Nausea, but in high concentrations it can also improve your Resistance.

🥤 Try the new **CactusCola**! A safe and delicious way to enjoy cactus with all its properties.

🔥 **Peeled cactus** can be cooked. It loses some of its properties, but it’s a great survival food.

🍹 **Ferment Cactus Rum** (integrated with **Brewin' and Chewin'**).


🎋 **Bamboo**

Bamboo is now an important part of your daily meals!

🍚 Vegan-friendly **rice with bamboo and seeds**
(Yes! Seeds are finally more useful!)

🥩 **Beef with bamboo and mushrooms** — a classic, nutritious dish.


🟢 **Slime**

Ever wondered what slime tastes like? Now you can find out!

🧪 Dense and sour flavor.

🔥 Smelt a Slime Ball in a furnace to process it.

🍞 Turn processed slime into **Slime Bread** (compatible with all bread recipes).

🍲 Placeable feast: **Slime Jelly**
Made with slime and kelp for extra consistency.
Take your portion with a bowl!

🍀 Slime grants the Luck effect — feel the power of green!


🌊 **Extra**

🥗 **Sea Salad** — A healthy way to add fresh kelp and sea pickles to your diet.

🍪 **Creeper & Cream** — A sweet treat that might become your new favorite snack!
